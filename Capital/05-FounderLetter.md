# Founder Letter (draft)

**Note on this document**: I wrote the facts below from what's actually built and verified in `OneHundred-Workspace`. I did not invent personal history, motivation, or voice on the founder's behalf — a fundraising letter's credibility rests on it being genuinely the founder's own words, not mine. Treat this as a factual skeleton to rewrite in your own voice before it goes out, not as a finished letter.

---

One Hundred started from a pattern that kept proving itself before there was one brand consistent enough to sell it under: take a business with something to sell, and in one working session, give it a real landing page, working payment, and an AI sales agent that actually knows the business — instead of a months-long custom build.

The first real proof of that is Osman Vergara: landing page, PayPal integration, and an AI agent answering questions and qualifying leads on WhatsApp and Telegram, all delivered from one reusable engine. That's not a mockup or a pitch — it's live.

What I'm asking for is COP $1,000,000 in initial capital — not investment, not equity, not a loan (see the risk disclosure for why that distinction matters and isn't just wording). It's enough to close the one real operational gap in what's already built (the sales agent needs to run on real infrastructure, not a laptop), do basic legal groundwork (domain, a trademark search), and prove the engine works a second time with a second real client.

I'd rather ask for less and be precise about what it buys than ask for more and pad it with a projection I can't back. If the honest answer to a question is "we don't know yet" or "that hasn't happened," that's what you'll get from me and from the AI agent representing the company — not a more comfortable version of the truth.

— [Founder name], One Hundred
