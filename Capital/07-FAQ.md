# Capital FAQ

**Is this an investment round?**
No. This is initial / launch capital, not equity, not a loan, not a securities offering. See `06-RiskDisclosure.md`.

**What happens to my money if this doesn't work?**
There is no repayment promise or guaranteed return of any kind. Read `06-RiskDisclosure.md` before considering participation — this isn't a formality, it's the actual answer.

**How would I participate in any future upside?**
Not defined yet. Any mechanism discussed is explicitly a **PROPOSED STRUCTURE — SUBJECT TO LEGAL REVIEW**, not an offer.

**Why COP $1,000,000 and not more or less?**
It's sized to fund the specific gaps that are actually blocking launch (persistent hosting for the AI agent, domain, basic legal search, a second client push) — see `03-UseOfFunds.md` — not to project growth or scale.

**Do you have a legal entity?**
Not yet. That's one of the gaps this capital is meant to help close.

**Can I talk to someone instead of reading documents?**
Yes — the AI agent on the live public site is grounded in the same facts as everything here, and will tell you honestly if it doesn't know something. There's no other contact channel set up yet.

**Is "One Hundred" a registered trademark?**
No, and this document makes no claim that it is. See `Launch/TrademarkChecklist.md` — LEGAL VERIFICATION REQUIRED.
