# Risk Disclosure

Read this before `02-CapitalDeck.md` slide 10 or any conversation about participation. Every point below is deliberate, not boilerplate.

## Legal framing — read carefully

The COP $1,000,000 being sought is described here, and must be described everywhere else this company talks about it publicly, as **"initial capital"** or **"launch capital"** — never as "investment," "equity," "a loan," or any transaction implying a **"guaranteed return."** No such structure has been legally defined or reviewed. Any mention anywhere of how a capital contributor might participate in future upside is a **PROPOSED STRUCTURE — SUBJECT TO LEGAL REVIEW**, not an offer, not a commitment, and not legally binding until reviewed and formalized by a lawyer. There is no repayment promise, no return promise, and no timeline for either.

## What's actually proven vs. what isn't

**Proven**: one real client (Osman Vergara) with a working landing page, payment integration, and AI sales agent, delivered from a reusable engine, in one session.

**Not proven**: that this repeats cheaply or predictably with a second, different client; that the engine generalizes beyond the "landing + payment + sales agent" shape; that there is market demand at any specific price point (no reference pricing exists yet); that the AI agent, in its current MVP/rule-based-fallback form, can reliably qualify leads or handle real sales conversations at volume.

## Legal and ownership risks

- **No legal entity currently exists** to receive capital, invoice, or hold IP. This must be resolved before any capital changes hands.
- **Trademark status is unverified** — "One Hundred" has not been legally cleared in any jurisdiction. See `Launch/TrademarkChecklist.md`.
- **Domain is not secured.**
- **An earlier, separate effort with real technical lineage to today's work has unresolved IP/ownership status** after the entity behind it dissolved. One Hundred does not claim ownership or continuity with it, and it is deliberately excluded from all public material until a lawyer resolves the question. If that resolution ever changes what One Hundred can claim, this document will be wrong until updated.

## Business risks

- One confirmed client is not a market. Nothing here should be read as evidence of repeatable demand.
- No pricing benchmark exists — the business does not yet know what it can reliably charge.
- The founder(s) and the venture's ability to execute without a legal entity, dedicated infrastructure, or a second client is unproven.

## What this document is not

Not a securities offering. Not a solicitation of investment. Not a guarantee of any kind. If you are evaluating a financial or legal instrument based on this document, stop and consult independent legal counsel — this document is intentionally not sufficient to make that decision on its own, by design.
