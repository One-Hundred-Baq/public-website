# One Hundred — Capital Deck (draft)

Structured as slides — each `##` is one slide. Public-facing draft; concise by design. No valuation slide, no projection slide, no "market size" slide with invented numbers — those are deliberately absent, not forgotten.

---

## 1. One Hundred

A technology venture company. We build and own reusable AI systems and digital assets.

## 2. The problem we build against

A small business or independent professional who needs to sell online has two bad options today: an expensive, slow custom build from a freelancer/agency, or a generic SaaS chatbot that doesn't know their business. Nothing packages "real sales presence + AI agent that actually knows the business" as one delivered thing.

## 3. What we've proven

One engine. One real, confirmed client (Osman Vergara): landing page, integrated payment, AI sales agent across WhatsApp and Telegram — delivered as one package, in one working session, from a reusable base. Not a pilot, not a mockup — live and working.

## 4. What we are not

Not an AI agency. Not a software factory. Not a dev studio selling hours. Not a SaaS platform (yet). One Hundred owns the engine; client work is proof it works, not the business itself.

## 5. The mechanism (why this scales)

Every delivery reuses the same engine and leaves it stronger — assets compound, hours don't. This is the actual case for scale, not a projection: it's how the second client will cost less effort than the first, and the tenth less than the second.

## 6. The MVP, live now

The public website itself is the product demo: an AI agent, grounded only in real facts about One Hundred, answering visitor questions in real time. Try it before reading further — it will tell you honestly what it doesn't know.
